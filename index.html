Resume

As a wise man once said "There are neither beginnings nor endings to the Wheel of Time" then maybe the time I started making decisions for my future is a beginning. Working at my first job after I finished my education at Sofia High School of Bread and Pastry I came to the realization that I need to invest into my future.

It started with applying at Sofia University "St. Kliment Ohridski". My Bachelor's degree is in Bulgarian Philology with courses in linguistics and literature in Bulgarian including 3 months of teaching Bulgarian language in high school. During my studies I was awarded 2nd place in a contest by Masthead studious for short story based on their project Earthrise. This landed me a gig at the studio as creative writer.

Later university networking landed me a job at Institute of Information and Communication Technologies at Bulgarian Academy of Science (IICT - BAS) at first as contractor, then staff member and research assistant. During the contractor period I was able to start and finish my Master's degree in Translation Studies at Sofia University "St. Kliment Ohridski" by translating the first 5 chapters from Timothy Zahn's Allegiance novel. Although I am using English every day I never managed to get proper certification. My only certificate is from 2010 for "intermediate" level from Britanica school.

As part of the Artificial Intelligence & Language Technologies department at IICT - BAS I was responsible for creating resources and knowledge representation for training and evaluation of systems. This included task such as: text alignment for machine translation; text annotation for information extraction from medical texts; named entity annotation for golden standart. Building Wortdnet for Bulgarian as part of CLADA-BG project for digital humanities; evaluating outputs of neural model for news article summarization. This work was done in XML with the use of CLaRK system. During the Wordnet creation I did some UI/UX testing for a new system for dictionary creation. All of this was supported with the proper research, writing and presentation of papers with the use of LaTeX. One of which was awarded with "Best Paper" at the RANLP 2019 Student Research Workshop. The department was hosts of events like international conference Recent Advances in Natural Language Processing (RANLP), the 30th edition of the European Summer School in Logic, Language and Information - ESSLLI 2018 and various workshops.

By the end of my stay at IICT - BAS I started learning Python for purposes of data science. I enlisted in the Python developer programme at SoftUni. When I reached OOP I involved myself with Pygame library and made my own project. For my successful completion of the programme it was required to present a Django web application. After I continued with the Machine Learning Programme starting with math for developers, transitioned into data science and machine learning and finished with deep learning.

After my departure from BAS I found work as data operations analyst at NielsenIQ in one of the output teams. My responsibility was by the use of internal tools to create database extracts, validate data deliveries and manage client access to extracts. After my first year I moved to deployment with the task to migrate and validate database extracts from legacy pipelines to the newest client solution platform.
